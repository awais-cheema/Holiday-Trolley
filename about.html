Our Story

Nearly Four Decades of Bespoke Travel

Holiday Trolley was founded in 1987 with a single, unwavering mission: to create holidays that genuinely change lives. From our very first booking to the thousands we arrange each year, every trip we curate is built around you — your dreams, your pace, your perfect escape.

We are one of the UK's most trusted luxury travel specialists, holding dual ATOL and ABTA accreditation — meaning every penny you spend with us is fully financially protected. Our team of expert travel consultants draw on decades of first-hand destination knowledge to build itineraries that no algorithm could replicate.

Whether you're dreaming of an overwater villa in the Maldives, a Big Five safari across Kenya's Masai Mara, or a tailored multi-centre adventure through Southeast Asia, we bring the same meticulous care and personal touch to every booking.

All the flight-inclusive holidays on this website are financially protected by the ATOL scheme. When you pay you will be supplied with an ATOL Certificate. Please ask for it and check to ensure that everything you booked (flights, hotels and other services) is listed on it. If you do receive an ATOL Certificate but all the parts of your trip are not listed on it, those parts will not be ATOL protected. Some of the flights on this website are also financially protected by the ATOL scheme, but ATOL protection does not apply to all flights. Holiday Trolley will provide you with information on the protection that applies in the case of each flight before you make your booking. If you do not receive an ATOL Certificate, then the booking will not be ATOL protected. Please see our booking conditions for information, or for more information about financial protection and the ATOL Certificate go to: www.caa.co.uk.
